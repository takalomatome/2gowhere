Table Mountain Complete Guide

Table Mountain is Cape Town's iconic landmark rising over the city with unique biodiversity and panoramic vistas. This guide helps you plan your ascent, whether by cable car or trail.

Top Hiking Routes

  • Platteklip Gorge: Direct and steep; 2–3 hours up.
  • Skeleton Gorge: Forested start in Kirstenbosch; connects to Maclear's Beacon.
  • India Venster: Scenic but includes scrambling—avoid in wet conditions.

Best Time to Visit

Early morning for cooler temperatures and clearer views. Avoid strong south-easter winds ("Cape Doctor")—cable car may close.

Safety & Preparation

  • Carry at least 1.5L water per person.
  • Use sun protection—UV is intense.
  • Stay on marked trails; sudden weather changes are common.
  • Tell someone your route and expected return time.

Wildlife & Flora

The mountain hosts hundreds of endemic plant species in the Cape Floral Region. Watch for dassies (rock hyrax) near viewpoints.

Photography Tips

  • Sunrise from the eastern edge for city glow.
  • Golden hour looking toward Lion's Head.
  • Use a polarizing filter to reduce ocean glare.

Responsible Visiting

Pack out all waste, respect wildlife distance, and avoid loud music on trails.
